The Kolathur Manifesto

Three Promises,
Ground-Checked. மூன்று வாக்குறுதிகள், தரையில் சரிபார்க்கப்பட்டவை.

Not a national wishlist. Three focus areas picked because they're the ones Kolathur residents actually bring up — at tea shops, at bus stops, at parent-teacher meetings. Tap each one to open it up.

01
Youth Employment & Skill Corridor
வேலைவாய்ப்பு பாதை
+
A local skill-training and placement cell tied directly to industries near Kolathur — closing the "degree but no job" gap that hits first-generation job seekers hardest.
Target: 6-month pilot with 3 local industrial partners
02
Flood-Proofing & Waste Management
வடிகால் மற்றும் சுத்தம்
+
A time-bound stormwater drain audit with a public, ward-wise progress tracker — so monsoon relief becomes a measurable commitment, not an annual apology.
Target: Public tracker live before next monsoon
03
Safe, Well-Lit Public Spaces
பாதுகாப்பான தெருக்கள்
+
Solar streetlight corridors and a women's safety patrol tie-up near transit points and coaching centers — so walking home after dark isn't a risk calculation.
Target: Priority corridors lit within first term
